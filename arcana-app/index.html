What a reading means

Tarot is a way to look at a situation from a new angle. Each card is a theme — not a fortune, and not a fixed fate. Where a card lands in a spread changes what it is talking about: the same card means something different as your past, your challenge, or your outcome. A reversed card is the same theme turned inward, blocked, or still forming. Read the cards together, not one by one — the overall reading underneath each spread is the part that matters most, especially if you don't read tarot yourself.

Kami Garden suits: South / Air = Swords, East / Fire = Wands, West / Water = Cups, North / Earth = Pentacles. Major Arcana = the big life themes. Minor Arcana = the everyday stuff — feelings, work, thoughts, and drive.

How to read it

One card = the theme to carry today. Three cards = where you've been, where you are, where it's heading. Celtic Cross = the full picture — present, challenge, past, future, what you want, what's underneath, and where it ends. Horseshoe = the same arc in seven cards, a little quicker. If a card feels off, that's useful too — ask what you're resisting.

For fun and reflection. Make big life calls with real information, not the cards.
