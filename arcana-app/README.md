# Arcana — v1.0.0

Tarot readings. Open `index.html` in any browser — no build step, no account, no internet needed.

## What it does

- Full 78-card deck: 22 Major Arcana and 56 Minor Arcana, upright and reversed meanings.
- Spreads: One Card, Three Cards (Past / Present / Future), Celtic Cross, Horseshoe, and Daily Card.
- Flip animations, a mandala tablecloth in 7 colours, and 3 swappable card-back designs.
- Every spread ends with a "Read together" summary explaining what the reading means.

Readings here are for reflection and fun.
