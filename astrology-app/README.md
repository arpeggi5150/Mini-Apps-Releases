# Astrology App — v1.3.0

Daily horoscope, zodiac signs, and birth chart. Open `index.html` in any browser — no build step, no account, no internet needed.

## What it does

- **Daily Horoscope** — pick your sign, get today's reading (Yesterday / Today / Tomorrow). Love, career, mood, lucky number and colour.
- **Zodiac Signs** — all 12 signs: dates, element, ruler, strengths, and compatibility.
- **Birth Chart** — enter birth date, time, and location. Sun is exact by date; Moon and Rising are simplified approximations and are labelled as such. Don't know your birth time or location? Say so — those parts stay incomplete, never faked.
- **Saved Charts** — save up to 100 charts on your device (browser storage only). Tap one to load it, ✕ to delete it.

Horoscopes and charts here are for fun and reflection.
